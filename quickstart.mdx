## Do I need to sign in?

Reading the website does not require an account. The guestbook asks for a supported identity provider before posting. The owner’s dashboard, private notes, uploads, and maintenance tools are restricted; guestbook authentication does not unlock them.

## Which newsletter am I joining?

The main website’s updates and the Muzix monthly listening snapshot have separate subscription flows. Confirm your address in the email you receive. Delivered newsletters provide an unsubscribe path.

## If something does not work

Check [website status](https://www.avrxt.dev/status) for availability information. Use the [contact page](https://www.avrxt.dev/contact) for a conversation. Never include private confirmation links or login tokens in a public message.
