Subscribe to main-site updates at [avrxt.dev/subscribe](https://www.avrxt.dev/subscribe). Muzix has a separate music snapshot subscription on [muzix.avrxt.dev](https://muzix.avrxt.dev).

## Confirm your address

After subscribing, open the confirmation email and follow its link. An address is not treated as a confirmed subscription until that step succeeds.

## Receiving updates

Main-site updates cover technical notes, tools, and occasional project news. The Muzix snapshot is a separate monthly listening newsletter.

## Leaving the list

Use the unsubscribe or email-preference link in a delivered message. Do not post private confirmation links, tokens, or recipient-specific unsubscribe links in public issues.

For data-handling details, read the relevant website’s privacy policy.
