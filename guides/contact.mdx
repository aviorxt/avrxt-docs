description: "How to contact avrxt, submit a personal or project inquiry, and understand delivery and receipt states."
---

Use [avrxt.dev/contact](https://www.avrxt.dev/contact) to send a message to the website owner. It is a personal inbox workflow, not a customer-support product.

## Before you submit

Include the reason for your message and enough context to respond. For a technical problem, give the public URL, approximate time, browser, and a sanitized reproduction. Do not include passwords, API keys, private session URLs, or identity documents.

## Accepted fields

The current form requires a name, email address, and message. Names are bounded to 120 characters, email addresses to 254, and contact messages to 5,000. Invalid or oversized requests are rejected before delivery.

## Delivery and receipts

Accepted contact messages are delivered to a configured private Discord channel. A receipt email is attempted afterwards. The submitted address is **not verified** by an inbox challenge in this flow. A receipt outage does not mean an accepted Discord delivery failed.

The interface states a review window of 72 hours. This is a personal review target, not a guaranteed support SLA.

## Avoid duplicate messages

If the form reports acceptance, do not submit repeatedly because a receipt has not arrived. Check spam and allow for delivery delays. For an explicit failure, check website status and try later.

<Note>The form uses same-origin submission. A gateway route does not make it an unrestricted third-party messaging API.</Note>
