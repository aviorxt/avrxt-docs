The [guestbook](https://www.avrxt.dev/guestbook) accepts signed messages when posting is enabled.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the guestbook">Browse the public messages and the current posting options.</Step>
  <Step title="Sign in when prompted">Use a supported identity provider shown by the guestbook.</Step>
  <Step title="Write a considered message">Follow the rules shown in the interface. Submission may be subject to automated moderation and rate limits.</Step>
</Steps>

Signing in does not grant access to administrative tools. Provider availability and posting controls can change.
