The [deletion-request page](https://www.avrxt.dev/delete-request) collects a scoped request for **manual review**. It does not automatically erase an account, media, or provider record.

## Identify the records

Supported scopes are newsletter, contact, account, media, and other. State your authority to request the review. Account and media requests need an identifier or public content URL so the owner can locate records. An “other” request needs explanatory details.

## Confirm the inbox

Verification links expire after 24 hours. Opening a link checks its state; an explicit confirmation action is required before the request is forwarded. Inbox verification alone does not prove ownership of every record named in the request.

## Review and outcome

The owner reviews scope, ownership, and relevant retention obligations. The interface’s 72-hour review window begins after the verified request is delivered. This is an intake workflow, not an automatic guarantee of erasure.

Do not send government identity documents through ordinary contact messages. Follow the published privacy policy for the current process and contact details.

## If a link expires

Use the form to request a fresh verification link. Do not publish or forward the expired token as a support example. Already-completed and unavailable links can have distinct states; follow the message shown in the interface.
