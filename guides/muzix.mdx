[Muzix](https://muzix.avrxt.dev) brings listening activity into one focused space.

## Listening data

The shared backend uses **Last.fm listening data**. A currently-playing track is only available when the scrobbling source reports it. Otherwise the interface can display the most recent known track and its available history.

<Note>Now playing depends on the connected player and scrobbling source. It is not a guarantee of continuous, second-by-second playback telemetry.</Note>

## Artwork

Track and artist artwork use the available music metadata and fallback providers. A missing image should not prevent the listening information from loading.

## Monthly snapshot

Muzix has its own email subscription flow. Readers confirm their address before receiving the monthly listening snapshot. Each message includes an unsubscribe option.
