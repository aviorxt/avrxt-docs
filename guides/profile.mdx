Open [me.avrxt.dev](https://me.avrxt.dev) to view the public AVRXT profile. Its content is managed separately from this documentation.

## What to expect

The profile presents the links and widgets the owner has enabled. Availability, music, gallery items, and other sections can change with the current profile configuration.

## Contact

For project conversations or questions, use the [contact page](https://www.avrxt.dev/contact). Viewing a profile does not require access to its administration tools.
